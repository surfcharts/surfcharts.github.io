How it works

Zones are set by where the chart sits versus its 200-week SMA:

ACCUMULATION≤ 1× 200W SMA
NEUTRAL1× – 2× 200W SMA
DISTRIBUTION≥ 2× 200W SMA

Why the 200W SMA?

The 200W SMA is the average weekly close of roughly the last 4 years. It filters out short-term swings and works as a slow long-term mean.

Historically, most major cycle lows have traded at or below it.

Important notes

A zone can last months or years. Use this for long-term context, not predictions.
